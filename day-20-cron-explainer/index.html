    Quick reference — the 5 fields

    * means "every". */15 means "every 15". 9-17 means "9 through 17". 1,15 means "1 and 15". Combine them: 0,30 9-17 * * MON-FRI runs at :00 and :30 past every hour from 9am to 5pm, Monday to Friday.

    Two gotchas: (1) If you restrict both day-of-month and day-of-week, cron runs when either matches — not both. (2) 0 0 * * 0 runs at midnight Sunday, not "every day". Day-of-week 0 and 7 both mean Sunday.

    Shortcuts: @yearly @monthly @weekly @daily @hourly @reboot (runs once at startup, not on a schedule).
